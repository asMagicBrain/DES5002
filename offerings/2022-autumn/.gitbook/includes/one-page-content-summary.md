The course covers robotics and social good, mathematical foundations, regression and classification, neural networks, convolutional networks, deep-learning architectures, generative design, soft robotics and AI risk. The Reachy Fusion project combines Fusion 360 training, mechanical design analysis, assembly, generative design, 3D printing and applications for social good. Students work in teams of four to five, with individual roles supporting technical drawings, assembly instructions, engineering analysis and documentation.
