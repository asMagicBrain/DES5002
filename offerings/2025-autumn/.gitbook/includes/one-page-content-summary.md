The course covers robotics and social good, soft robotics, humanoids, data and machine learning, AI applications in sound, image, text and body, and AI risk. Lectures are complemented by case-study workshops, tutorials, an interim review and a final review. Students review three cases individually and work in teams of three to propose a robot for social good, communicating hardware design, intelligence and human–robot interaction through two posters.
