The course covers robotics and social good, generative design, soft robotics, human–machine interaction, data and machine learning, and AI applications in sound, image, text and body. Case-study workshops, guest lectures, tutorials and reviews support a team project on humanoid robots and their applications for social good. The Reachy Fusion project uses Autodesk Fusion 360 for design analysis and asks teams of three to communicate hardware design and applications using robotics and large language models.
