# DES5002 — Designing Robots for Social Good

DES5002 explores how robotics can address human and societal needs. The course
connects the technical foundations of robotic systems with the ethical questions
involved in designing robots for social good. Through case studies and design
challenges, students examine robot design, materials, modeling and interaction
with the physical environment, and apply these ideas to socially meaningful
applications.
